A playful procedural book-title generator inspired by the familiar *The [Occupation] of [Place]* naming convention in historical fiction.

Combining curated lists of occupations and locations, it generates imaginary novels complete with their own visual presentation. A small creative-coding experiment in language, randomness and literary clichés.
